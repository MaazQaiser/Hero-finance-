Build notes for Appifest

v2. Behaviour matters more than styling. Every rule below is deliberate.

Structure

  • Six named sections. Never show a total question count and never show a time estimate.
  • One question per screen, one page. Client side transitions only, no reload.
  • All questions in a section stay together. Employment and address are never split.
  • Section rail fills within the current segment. The car marker travels the whole journey.
  • Section arrival card shows on entering sections two to six. 900ms, tap to skip. A/B test whether it stays.

Deposit

  • Deposit sits in section one as a one tap band. It changes the amount of credit and therefore the affordability assessment.
  • Pass the band to Autoconvert with the proposal. The indicative decision must be calculated on budget minus deposit, not on an arbitrary figure.
  • Part exchange is deliberately excluded from this journey. It belongs after approval, on vehicle selection, as a yes or no that captures registration and mileage only, routed to the team or SCI to value.

Interaction

  • Choice questions auto advance on tap. No Continue button on those screens.
  • Selection plays a fill sweep and fires a haptic where supported. This interaction happens fourteen times.
  • Typed fields that belong together share a screen: name, employer and job title, postcode and address.
  • Correct keyboard per field: numeric for mobile and income, email keyboard for email, native date picker for date of birth.
  • Validation fires on submit or blur only. Never on load.
  • Back never loses an answer.
  • Every answer posts to the backend as it is given, so an abandoned application is still a callable lead.

Conditional logic

  • Employer, job title and time in role are skipped for retired, student, unemployed, receiving benefits and homemaker.
  • Under three years at current address triggers a previous address block. Loop until three years is captured.
  • Joint applicant selection captures the second applicant after the main applicant completes.

Tone

  • Warm and open through sections one to three, where nothing sensitive is being asked.
  • Calm and quiet through address, income and consent. Smaller headlines, white background, no flourish.
  • No fake encouragement messages anywhere. The section name does that job honestly.
  • One trust line per section, matched to the question being asked.
  • First name used once, on the decision screen.

Decision screen (compliance critical)

  • Approved in principle, never approved. Subject to full underwriting, ID, proofs and the vehicle.
  • Any APR shown must sit with a full representative example.
  • Anchor on the budget and deposit the customer gave, never an invented approval figure.
  • Matching stock pulled inline. The highest intent moment in the journey must not be a dead end.
  • A decline and a refer path are still to be built. Decline will carry real volume on this traffic.
  • Final wording and figures go through FCA compliance review before launch.
